Automated Wire Cutter

An Arduino-driven rig that measures, feeds and cuts wire to a programmed length with repeatable precision — replacing a slow manual process with a menu-driven machine.

Year2024 – 2025

Overview

What it does

The wire cutter automates a repetitive, error-prone task. An operator enters a length and quantity on the LCD menu, and the machine feeds and cuts each piece accurately — with an emergency-stop and safety interlock so it halts instantly and predictably.

Motion & Drivers

Stepper motors with DRV8825 microstepping drivers deliver smooth, accurate feed and cut motion.

Operator UI

A menu-driven LCD interface lets an operator set length and quantity without touching code.

Safety Logic

An emergency-stop and interlock bring the machine to a controlled, predictable halt.

Calibration

Tuned acceleration and step calibration balance cutting speed against accuracy.
