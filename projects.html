University Projects

Automated Wire Cutter & Stripper

My final-year project (Module 23WSC325): a compact, low-cost machine that automates wire feeding, cutting and stripping. Built around an Arduino Uno with twin DRV8825-driven stepper axes, a rotary-encoder and LCD interface, and a milled-aluminium and 3D-printed chassis — feeding accurate to ±2 mm across 0.5–4.0 mm² wire.

Stepper Motor Control System

Testing and control framework for Sanmotion and Motech stepper motors — matching each motor to a DRV8825 driver, setting current limits by tuning VREF, and calibrating microstepping and step-per-mm resolution for smooth, accurate motion.
